The first-timers with the top PWin%

French Sand (Eddie Kenneally, 6/1) has the highest PWin% of the first-timers at 11%, followed by For My Sake (20/1, 4%) and Teacher (30/1, 3%). Eddie Kenneally is 3 for 24 with first-time starters in dirt sprints here (12%, +45% ROI). Reina Gonzalez and Jerry Dixon Jr. have no first-time winners in dirt sprints here in five years.

Best trainer record on the card

Brendan Walsh's Rainbow Lightning (8/1, PWin 3%) has the best trainer record among today's first-timers: 7 for 39, in the money 44% of the time, +37% ROI. The others with a record: Wayne Mackey (Confidence) 2 for 2, +2027%; Eddie Kenneally (French Sand) 3 for 24, +45%; Dallas Stewart (At Full Sail) 1 for 9, −36%; Robert Medina (Dialed) 1 for 16, −11%; John Ennis (Goodbye Girl) 2 for 48, −23%.
