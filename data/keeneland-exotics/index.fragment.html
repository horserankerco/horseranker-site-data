ITM % is how often their horses finished first, second or third. Win ROI, Place ROI and Show ROI are the return on $2 to win, to place or to show on every start. Exacta Top ROI is the return on a $1 exacta with the horse on top of every other horse (it pays when the horse wins), and Exacta Bottom ROI is the return with every other horse on top of it (it pays when the horse runs second). Each costs $1 for every other horse in the field: $9 in a 10-horse field. Avg Trifecta Pays is what a $1 trifecta paid on average when the horse finished in the top three. Power Box ROI is what a trifecta power box would have returned if you paired the horse with one other horse and found one of the other two horses in the top three: 1/2/ALL, 1/ALL/2, 2/1/ALL, 2/ALL/1, ALL/1/2 and ALL/2/1, six combinations for each of the other horses in the field, $48 in a 10-horse field. It is the ceiling for that ticket, and it assumes your second horse is right. ML is the morning line. PML is our Projected Final Odds and PWin% is our Projected Chance of Winning.
