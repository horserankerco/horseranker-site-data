Each costs $1 for every other horse in the field: $9 in a 10-horse field. Avg Trifecta Pays is what a $1 trifecta paid on average when the horse finished in the top three. It is the ceiling for that ticket, and it assumes your second horse is right. ML is the morning line. PML is our Projected Final Odds and PWin% is our Projected Chance of Winning.
